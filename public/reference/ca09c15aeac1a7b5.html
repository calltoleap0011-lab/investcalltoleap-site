Road to $100K: Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the Road to $100K Program for?

Road to $100K (or RT1) is for beginners who want to start investing the right way without gambling, day trading, or overcomplicating things.
If you feel overwhelmed, unsure where to start, or stuck watching videos without taking action, RT1 was built for you.

Do I need any investing experience?

No. RT1 is designed for people starting from zero.
We assume no prior knowledge and walk you through everything step by step, using plain language and simple examples.

How much money do I need to get started?

You don’t need a lot of money to begin.
RT1 focuses on building the habit, the system, and the foundation first. Many members start with small amounts and scale up over time as their confidence grows.

How much time does this take each week?

For the first two weeks, plan for about 30 minutes a day to get your accounts set up and everything in place.

After that, most members spend 1–2 hours per week engaging in the community and joining the group coaching calls.

Is this investing or trading?

RT1 is long-term investing, not day trading.
We focus on building real portfolios, managing risk responsibly, and staying consistent through market ups and downs. No hype. No shortcuts.

What happens if the market drops?

Market drops are normal.
RT1 teaches you how to think long term, manage risk, and stay calm during volatility so you don’t panic or make emotional decisions.

What makes RT1 different from free YouTube videos?

Free content is scattered. RT1 is structured.
Instead of guessing what to watch next, RT1 gives you a clear roadmap, so you know exactly what to do, in the right order, with confidence.

Is RT1 the same as your 1:1 coaching program?

No. RT1 is the foundation.

If you’re brand new to investing or still building confidence, RT1 is the best place to start. It’s designed to help you understand investing, build a real portfolio, and make decisions on your own.

For people who already have capital and want hands-on guidance, that’s a separate 1:1 coaching program and requires an application.

If coaching makes sense for your situation, the quiz will point you in the right direction. Coaching details are only shared after an application to ensure it’s a good fit.
